1. The “I” and “Want” mirror

Twelve statements to see which voice is speaking louder in you. Answer from how things are, not how you wish they were.

1 = Rarely5 = Very often

  1. Once I get what I wanted, I quickly want the next thing.

  2. In an argument, winning matters more to me than understanding.

  3. I buy or keep more than I actually use.

  4. I find it hard to apologise first.

  5. I feel uneasy when others have more than I do.

  6. I put my own needs ahead of those of the people close to me.

  7. I crave attention, praise or fame.

  8. I am easily hurt by criticism.

  9. I find it hard to feel satisfied with what I have.

  10. I rarely truly put myself in someone else’s place.

  11. I want more power or influence over other people.

  12. I often think I am more right than the people around me.

2. What I truly want

Write down the things you want. For each, ask: does it suit me? Keep what suits you; let go of the wishes that do not.

True happiness comes from simple, practical things. Look at your “keep” list: you probably have part of most of it already.
