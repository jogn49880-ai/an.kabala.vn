“I” and “Want”: the two roots of suffering

The Peaceful Way is founded on the recognition that all suffering in life originates from greed and the ego.

I · Ego

“I must be more.”

An inflated ego leads to selfishness, arrogance and a lack of empathy: putting your own needs above everything, which breeds conflict.

  • Wounded pride, envy
  • Hard to apologise, hard to listen
  • Constant comparison

Drop “I” to find the meaning of life

Want · Greed

“Not enough.”

Excessive desire for possessions, power or recognition. Never satisfied with what you have, always wanting more.

  • Impatience, fear of loss
  • Overwork, buying to compensate
  • Reaching the goal and still feeling short

Drop “Want” to recognise true happiness

Dropping “Want” does not mean having no wishes. It means letting go of the wishes that do not suit you, so you can see what you truly want.

The Circle of Hiếu Đạo

To drop “I” and “Want”, you need to understand the Circle of Hiếu Đạo, with An, peace, at its centre. Select each word to see what it means and how to practise it.

An Peace · root and centre

Source
“An” is the root and the centre. Everything good and of real value in life begins from a peaceful, still inner state.
Explanation
The circle always begins and ends at An. Only when you truly touch An do you enter the journey of discovering yourself.
Practice today
Peaceful Meditation: count slowly from 1 to 6 until the mind is empty.
Ask yourself
Right now, is my mind at rest?

Hiếu Filial devotion

Source
Respecting and loving one’s parents, elders, and those who have done one good.
Explanation
Hiếu is not something forced by duty or by pressure from money and status. It is a serene inner state that lets your parents feel at ease, and through that you find peace yourself.
Practice today
Call a parent, or someone who once helped you, and listen.
Ask yourself
Are my parents at ease about me?

Đạo The Way

Source
Pursuing the path of ethics and ideals in life.
Explanation
A way of living led by inner peace itself. Someone who lives by it is not shaken by outside forces and stays steady through hardship.
Practice today
Write down your three principles for living.
Ask yourself
Is this on my path?

Đức Virtue

Source
Developing and maintaining noble moral qualities.
Explanation
Formed from experience and deep self-awareness. Not only outward good qualities, but a balanced soul that is kind and sincere with everyone.
Practice today
Choose one quality to train this week.
Ask yourself
With whom was I sincere today?

Phúc Blessing

Source
Creating and experiencing blessings and happiness from ethical action.
Explanation
Not only material prosperity, but serenity, fulfilment, harmony in every relationship, and deep meaning in life.
Practice today
Do one kind thing nobody needs to know about.
Ask yourself
Which of my relationships needs mending?

Tài Abundance through contentment

Source
Attaining wealth through contentment, not only in money but in every aspect of life.
Explanation
When Blessing is complete, Abundance comes naturally. It is not only material: it is richness of mind and feeling, and the ability to share with the community.
Practice today
Write down what you already have enough of, and what you will share.
Ask yourself
For me, how much is enough?

In daily life

The Peaceful Way not only helps individuals grow morally and spiritually; it also aims at a peaceful, harmonious society.

Yourself
Under stress, ask quickly: is this “I” or “Want” speaking?
Family
One phone-free meal a week. Live so that your parents are at ease.
Education
A minute of silence to start class. Teach children “enough” before “more”.
Work
Whatever you do, be wholly in it; do not think about the result or the end.
Leadership
Pause a minute before major decisions. Admit mistakes openly.
Digital life
Contentment with attention: ask “what am I opening this for?” first.

What the Peaceful Way is not

  • Fortune-telling or prophecy: choice matters more than fate.
  • A substitute for medicine or psychotherapy.
  • An exclusive creed: you keep your own religion.
  • The veneration of any individual.

Frequently asked questions

If I drop “Want”, will I have any motivation left?

Yes. Dropping “Want” means letting go of greed and of wishes that do not suit you. When you know yourself, you see more clearly what you truly want.

Does dropping “I” mean erasing myself?

No. What is let go is the inflated ego: selfishness, arrogance, lack of empathy. You are still you, lighter.

Where do I start?

With the sentence “I want happiness”. Read the first teaching, then use the “I” and “Want” mirror. Peaceful Meditation is a supporting method for when your mind needs settling.

Is the Peaceful Way a religion?

Today it is a practical philosophy of living. It does not ask you to give up any belief.

My family is complicated. How does Hiếu apply?

Hiếu is inner serenity, not obligation. It never obliges anyone to endure harm.

Is this related to the Buddhist master Dao’an?

No. Kabala’s Đạo An is a modern concept; the shared name is a coincidence.
