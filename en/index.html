Đạo An · Peaceful Way

Peace first. Everything else follows.

The Peaceful Way is a philosophy of living that helps you recognise your own true happiness and live a meaningful life, right in the middle of work, family and everyday change.

Begin in 5 minutesRead the white paper

An

The Peaceful Way in four sentences

  1. DiagnosisAll suffering in life grows from two roots: Greed and Ego.
  2. CoreThe remedy is Contentment: seeing and being at ease with what you already have.
  3. PathKnow yourself, then true happiness, then a meaningful life.
  4. StructureThe Circle of Hiếu Đạo: Peace gives rise to Gratitude, the Way, Virtue, Blessing, Abundance, and returns to Peace.

The two roots of suffering

Most unrest comes not from circumstances but from two inner voices. Recognising them is half the journey.

Greed

“Not enough.”

Excessive desire for possessions, power or recognition. Never satisfied with what you have, always wanting more.

  • Impatience, fear of loss
  • Overwork, buying to compensate
  • Reaching the goal and still feeling short

Remedy: Contentment

Ego

“I must be more.”

An inflated ego leads to selfishness, arrogance and a lack of empathy: putting your own needs above everything, which breeds conflict.

  • Wounded pride, envy
  • Hard to apologise, hard to listen
  • Constant comparison

Remedy: Humility and gratitude

The task is to recognise and master them: reduce unnecessary desires and live humbly, and you find inner peace and harmony with others.

The Path to Peace

Three steps, in order. Without self-knowledge, happiness is borrowed. Without contentment, “meaning” easily becomes another costume for the Ego.

  1. 1

    Know yourself

    Become aware of your own needs, wants and limits.

    What do I really need? Where are my limits?

  2. 2

    True happiness

    Find joy and satisfaction in simple, practical things.

    Which simple things are nourishing me?

  3. 3

    A meaningful life

    Seek and fulfil your purpose through action and relationships with others.

    What do I live to give, and to whom?

The Circle of Hiếu Đạo

An endless cycle that always begins and ends at An, peace. Select each word to see what it means and how to practise it.

An Peace · the centre

Source
“An” is the root and the centre. Everything good and of real value in life begins from a peaceful, still inner state.
Explanation
A mind that is steady, clear and sufficient whether or not things go your way. Not the absence of waves, but somewhere to stand when they come.
Practice today
Five minutes of Peaceful Meditation before picking up your phone.
Ask yourself
Right now, what am I actually lacking?

Hiếu Filial devotion

Source
Respecting and loving one’s parents, elders, and those who have done one good.
Explanation
Hiếu is not something forced by duty or by pressure from money and status. It is a serene inner state that lets your parents feel at ease, and through that you find peace yourself.
Practice today
Call a parent, or someone who once helped you, and listen.
Ask yourself
Are my parents at ease about me?

Đạo The Way

Source
Pursuing the path of ethics and ideals in life.
Explanation
A way of living led by inner peace itself. Someone who lives by it is not shaken by outside forces and stays steady through hardship.
Practice today
Write down your three principles for living.
Ask yourself
Is this on my path?

Đức Virtue

Source
Developing and maintaining noble moral qualities.
Explanation
Formed from experience and deep self-awareness. Not only outward good qualities, but a balanced soul that is kind and sincere with everyone.
Practice today
Choose one quality to train this week.
Ask yourself
What did I do right today, and what should I mend?

Phúc Blessing

Source
Creating and experiencing blessings and happiness from ethical action.
Explanation
Not only material prosperity, but serenity, fulfilment, harmony in every relationship, and deep meaning in life.
Practice today
Do one kind thing nobody needs to know about.
Ask yourself
What am I leaving for those who come after?

Tài Abundance through contentment

Source
Attaining wealth through contentment, not only in money but in every aspect of life.
Explanation
When Blessing is complete, Abundance comes naturally. It is not only material: it is richness of mind and feeling, and the ability to share with the community.
Practice today
Write down your “enough number”, and the share you will give.
Ask yourself
For me, how much is enough?

In daily life

The Peaceful Way is designed for people living ordinary lives.

Yourself
Under stress, ask quickly: is this Greed or Ego speaking?
Family
One phone-free meal a week. Teach children “enough” before “more”.
Education
A minute of silence to start class. Name feelings; thank those who helped.
Work
Ethics first, profit second, and profit has a point of enough.
Leadership
Pause a minute before major decisions. Admit mistakes openly.
Digital life
Contentment with attention: ask “what am I opening this for?” first.

In dialogue with other traditions

The Peaceful Way inherits the spirit of several traditions and rearranges it for people living ordinary lives today.

What the Peaceful Way is not

  • Fortune-telling or prophecy: choice matters more than fate.
  • A substitute for medicine or psychotherapy.
  • An exclusive creed: you keep your own religion.
  • The veneration of any individual.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Peaceful Way a religion?

Today it is a practical philosophy of living. It does not ask you to give up any belief. The white paper describes what would need to be built if the community wants to go further.

Will contentment kill my motivation?

No. Contentment comes from clarity, not from giving up. You still do your best; you are just not bound to the result.

I live far from my parents, or my family is complicated. How does Hiếu apply?

Hiếu is gratitude to your roots, which include teachers, benefactors and community. It never obliges anyone to endure harm.

How much time do I need each day?

Start with 5 minutes of Peaceful Meditation in the morning and three lines of contentment in the evening.

Is this related to the Buddhist master Dao’an?

No. Kabala’s Đạo An is a modern concept; the shared name is a coincidence.

Đạo An (the Peaceful Way) is a philosophy initiated by Kabala, Vietnam. Source text: Mr.33, Việt Trì, 10 April 2025.
