Lesson 11 / 12 · Destination and method

Know yourself: one destination

Three purposes but one destination: reach self-understanding, and the other two are reached as well.

Source: Đạo An, by Mr.33.

The Peaceful Way names three purposes: knowing yourself, true happiness, and a meaningful life. But they are not three peaks to climb one after another. There is only one destination: knowing yourself.

Knowing yourself means being aware of your own needs, wants and limits. When we are at peace, that understanding comes by itself. And from it the other two open:

  • We know what we truly want, and so recognise true happiness.
  • We know what we need to let go of, the ego, and so find the meaning of life.

True happiness is joy and satisfaction in simple, practical things. The meaning of life is found through action and relationships with others. Both are fruit. Self-understanding is the root.

Practice. Use the “I and Want mirror” on the Know yourself page.

Ask yourself. What do I really need? What do I want? Where are my limits?
