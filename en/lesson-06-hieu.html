Lesson 6 / 12 · The Circle of Hiếu Đạo

Hiếu: filial devotion

Hiếu is a serene inner state that lets one's parents feel at ease, and through that we too find real peace in our soul.

Source: Đạo An, by Mr.33.

Hiếu is respecting and loving one's parents, elders, and those who have done one good. It is the first word of the circle, where peace steps out of the inner life and into the closest relationship a person has.

The Peaceful Way understands Hiếu in its own way. It is not something forced by duty. Nor is it measured by the money or status we bring home. Its measure is: are my parents at ease about me?

When a child lives peacefully, kindly and steadily, the parents are at ease. And when the parents are at ease, the child's heart is lighter. Hiếu is therefore a two-way road of peace.

Hiếu never obliges anyone to endure harm. For someone whose family circumstances are difficult, it can still be practised with those who have done them good.

Practice. Call a parent, or someone who once helped you. Ask how they are, then listen.

Ask yourself. What about me is keeping my parents from being at ease?
