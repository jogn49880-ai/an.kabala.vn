Đạo An · Peaceful Way

Peaceful Meditation

Thiền An joins the stillness of meditation with the outlook of the Peaceful Way. No equipment and no experience needed.

Four steps

  1. 1

    Be still

    Sit comfortably, back naturally upright, shoulders and face relaxed.

  2. 2

    Observe

    Notice the breath; notice thoughts arriving and leaving. See which are Greed and which are Ego.

  3. 3

    Return

    When the mind runs off, count slowly from 1 to 6 and repeat, or silently repeat a short phrase that is meaningful to you.

  4. 4

    Remain

    When the mind has settled, stop counting and rest in that quiet.

The source text suggests “A Di Đà Phật” (Amitabha) or “Om Mani Padme Hum”. You may use a phrase from your own tradition, or simply the word “An”.

Meditation in daily activity

Working
Give the task your whole attention, feeling each movement and each breath.
Eating
Take the first three bites in silence, grateful to those who made the meal.
Walking
Count steps in a rhythm of 1 to 6, feeling your feet touch the ground.
When angry
Stop, and count one round of 1 to 6 before you speak.

A 21-day plan

A suggested way to begin. About 10 minutes a day.

Week 1 · Know yourself

5 minutes of meditation each morning. Each evening note one moment of Greed and one of Ego from the day.

Week 2 · True happiness

Add the contentment journal each evening. Choose one “no-more day”: no shopping, no aimless scrolling.

Week 3 · A meaningful life

One act of gratitude or kindness each day. At the weekend, write your three principles for living.

If you are in a mental-health crisis or have a health condition, please seek professional care. Peaceful Meditation is a support, not a substitute.
