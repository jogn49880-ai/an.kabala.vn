Đạo An · Peaceful Way

Peaceful Meditation

Thiền An joins meditation with the Peaceful Way. Its aim is not only stillness and balance of mind, but a deeper view of happiness and the meaning of life.

Step 1 · Begin the spiritual journey

Spirituality is the path by which a person can understand themselves, and from there find the meaning and purpose of life.

Source

Spirituality is not only about ethics and religion. It includes personal growth, the ability to connect with the world around you, and recognising how much a meaningful purpose matters.

Step 2 · Recognise the value of stillness

Stillness is the state of the soul when there is no distraction and no activity of thought. The source text names six values:

  1. Peace and relaxation
  2. Stronger focus on the present
  3. Deeper awareness of yourself and the world
  4. Quiet space to work through difficulty
  5. A source of creativity
  6. Growth of the soul

The body always has a way of restoring its own balance. When we are too active (talking, thinking, moving), energy goes to those things. Only when we stop does that energy turn to healing within. The most basic effect of stillness is to re-establish balance, which is to say, healing.

Step 3 · Practise

  1. Any time

    At home or outside, in quiet or in noise, while doing something or doing nothing.

  2. Any posture

    Walking, standing, lying down, sitting.

  3. Choose a way that suits you

    When doing nothing, or caught up in thoughts

    • Count silently and slowly 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, over and over, until the mind is empty.
    • Or silently and slowly repeat “A Di Đà Phật” (Amitabha).
    • Or silently and slowly repeat “Om Mani Padme Hum”.
    • You may also create a method of your own, or use a common one.

    When working

    • Give the task your whole attention, feeling each movement and each breath. Do not think about the result or the end.
    • What matters is being fully present, here and now.

  4. Relax the mind

    When the mind starts to wander, bring it back to where it was focused.

  5. Listen

    After that you do not have to do anything at all. Just listen to your own body and mind.

Keep the practice going, continuously.

Meditation timer

Watch the circle and count silently from 1 to 6. Once the mind is quiet, let the timer run and simply listen.
