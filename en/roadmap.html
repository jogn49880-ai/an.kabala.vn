Your 21 days

A suggested practice, about 10 minutes a day. Add a few minutes of meditation each day if your mind needs settling.

Week 1 · I want happiness

Read the first four teachings. Use the “I” and “Want” mirror. Write three enoughs each evening.

Week 2 · The Circle of Hiếu Đạo

One teaching a day, from An to Tài, doing that lesson’s practice. Use the “My circle” tool.

Week 3 · Know yourself

Do “What I truly want” and “What I let go”. One act of Hiếu or kindness each day. Invite one person to start a Circle of An with you.
