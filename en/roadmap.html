Your 21 days

A suggested practice, about 10 minutes a day. The three weeks are not three separate stages: all of them return to An.

Week 1 · An

Meditate every day, once while still and once while working. Just count, return, then listen.

Week 2 · Drop “Want”

Keep meditating. Add the contentment journal each evening and do “What I truly want”.

Week 3 · Drop “I”

Keep meditating. One act of Hiếu or kindness each day. Do “What I let go” and “My circle”.

Where this site is going

The plan for making the Peaceful Way reachable by anyone in the world. Later phases are proposals awaiting Kabala’s decision.

  1. Live

    Foundation

    The source philosophy, the Circle of Hiếu Đạo, Peaceful Meditation and the white paper, in Vietnamese and English.

  2. Live

    Tools for knowing yourself

    The “I” and “Want” mirror, what I want, what I let go, my circle, the contentment journal, Balance. Data stays on your device.

  3. Next

    Global reach

    More languages (Chinese, Spanish, Hindi, French, Japanese…), install as an app and use offline, a guided audio meditation, the original illustrations and podcast.

  4. Next

    Practising together

    A day-by-day 21-day course, a guide to starting a “Circle of An” of 5–12 people, and kits for families, schools and workplaces.

  5. Later

    Community and stewardship

    A library of practitioners’ stories, training for guides, a council that looks after the content, open finances.

Đạo An (the Peaceful Way) is a philosophy initiated by Kabala, Vietnam. Source texts: Mr.33, 2024–2025.

The core teachings are always free. The Peaceful Way is not a substitute for medical or psychological care.
