Lesson 10 / 12 · The Circle of Hiếu Đạo

Tài: abundance

Tài here is not merely material; it is richness and variety of mind and feeling, and the ability to share with the community.

Source: Đạo An, by Mr.33.

Tài is attaining wealth through contentment, not only in money but in every aspect of life. When Phúc is complete, Tài comes naturally.

This order is the most thought-provoking thing about the circle. Most of us put wealth first: earn the money, then think about ethics, family and peace. The Peaceful Way puts it last. Wealth comes afterwards, and it is measured by enough, not by much.

Tài is also wider than money. Understanding is wealth. A rich emotional life is wealth. And the ability to share with the community is its highest form, because only someone who feels they have enough can share.

Contented abundance returns us to An, and the circle begins again.

Practice. Share one thing you have enough of: time, knowledge, or money.

Ask yourself. In what way am I rich without ever having noticed?
