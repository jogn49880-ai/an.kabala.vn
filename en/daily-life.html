Đạo An · Peaceful Way

The Peaceful Way in daily life

Ten hard moments that come to everyone. For each: which voice is speaking, how to see it, what to do right away, and one question.

When angry

Voice speaking: I
See
Anger is usually the ego being touched: “I was disrespected”, “I must be right”.
Do now
Stop. Count silently once from 1 to 6 before you speak.
Ask yourself
Am I defending what is right, or defending my image?

When envious, comparing

Voice speaking: I and Want
See
“Want” looks at what others have; “I” measures itself against them.
Do now
Write today’s three enoughs. Switch off the source of comparison for a day.
Ask yourself
What they have: does it even suit me?

When worried about money

Voice speaking: Want
See
Some worry is real and needs real action. Some is a “not enough” that never stops.
Do now
Write down your enough number. Do today whatever is within reach.
Ask yourself
Am I truly short, or afraid of being short?

In conflict with parents

Voice speaking: I
See
Usually two egos meeting. Hiếu is putting your parents at ease, not beating them.
Do now
Listen once to the end without arguing. Say what you are grateful for before what you disagree with.
Ask yourself
What are my parents worried about for me?

When exhausted

Voice speaking: Want
See
With too much activity, no energy is left for healing within.
Do now
Stop. Five minutes of doing nothing, just listening to your body.
Ask yourself
Am I doing more because I need to, or because of “not enough”?

When you fail

Voice speaking: I
See
The thing that went wrong is an event. “I am a failure” is the ego telling a story.
Do now
Write one thing learned and one thing you will mend.
Ask yourself
Does it hurt because it failed, or because others saw?

When grieving

Voice speaking: Not your fault
See
Sorrow at losing someone is neither greed nor ego. It is love.
Do now
Let yourself grieve. Sit still and listen. Find someone to be with. If it does not ease, see a professional.
Ask yourself
What has that person left in me?

When lonely

Voice speaking: I
See
The meaning of life is found through action and relationships with others.
Do now
Do one kind thing for one person today. Make one phone call.
Ask yourself
Who needs me that I have not noticed?

When you do not know what you want

Voice speaking: I and Want
See
When we are at peace, we understand ourselves. An unsettled mind cannot see clearly.
Do now
A few minutes of stillness, then the “What I truly want” tool.
Ask yourself
Is this what I want, or what others want for me?

When you succeed and are praised

Voice speaking: I
See
This is when the ego inflates most easily.
Do now
Thank, specifically, the people who helped. Share part of what you have.
Ask yourself
Does this success make me lighter or heavier?

If you are in crisis, or have thoughts of harming yourself, contact family, a doctor or a helpline where you live right away. The Peaceful Way is a support, not a substitute for professional care.
