What newcomers usually ask, and what each word means.

About the Peaceful Way

What is Đạo An?

Đạo An, the Peaceful Way, is a system of thought and a philosophy of living whose purpose is to help people recognise their own true happiness and live a meaningful life.

Who is the author?

Mr.33, a Vietnamese writer within the Kabala ecosystem. The text on Peaceful Meditation was written in Hanoi on 1 November 2024; the text on Đạo An in Việt Trì on 10 April 2025.

Is it a religion?

It is a practical philosophy of living. It does not ask you to give up any belief and it venerates no one.

I follow another religion. Can I practise this?

Yes. You keep your own religion. When meditating you may use words from your own tradition.

Is this related to the Buddhist master Dao’an?

No. This is a modern concept; the shared name is a coincidence.

About “I want happiness”

Why start with this sentence?

Because it is what everyone wants, and the sentence already contains the answer: drop “I”, drop “Want”, and only Happiness remains.

If I drop “Want”, will I have any motivation left?

Yes. What is let go is greed, and wishes for what does not suit you. When you know yourself, you see more clearly what you truly want.

Does dropping “I” mean erasing myself?

No. What is let go is the inflated ego: selfishness, arrogance, lack of empathy.

How do I know a wish “does not suit me”?

Ask three questions: does it match a real need of mine? Is it beyond my limits? Once I have it, will I stop?

What does “right now” mean when my life is hard?

The Peaceful Way does not deny real hardship; what must be done still must be done. “Right now” means you need not wait until everything is settled before you are allowed to feel at peace.

About practice

Where should I start?

Read the teaching “I want happiness”, then use the “I” and “Want” mirror. Tonight, write three enoughs.

Is meditation required?

No. Peaceful Meditation is a supporting method for the journey towards peace within. You can practise at any time, in any posture.

How much time each day?

About ten minutes: a few minutes of stillness and three lines of contentment. The rest is how you live your day.

Is contentment the same as resignation?

No. A contented person still does their best; they just do not need the result before they are allowed to feel at peace.

My family is complicated. How does Hiếu apply?

Hiếu is inner serenity, not obligation, and it never obliges anyone to endure harm. You can practise it with those who have done you good.

Do I need to join a group?

No. But practice with others tends to last; see the Circles page.

About this site

Does the site charge, or collect data?

No. Everything is free. What you write stays in your browser; there are no accounts.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. After the first visit the site stores itself for offline use, and it can be installed like an app.

Does the Peaceful Way replace a doctor?

No. If you have a physical or mental health problem, please see a professional.
